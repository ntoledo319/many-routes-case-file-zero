Case File Zero: The Elm Street Cash Drawer

Saturday, 7:00 p.m. Nell Ambrose, the Elm Street ticket clerk, opens the office safe drawer to bank the day’s fares and finds $40 missing. The drawer was locked at 5:31 p.m. with $112.40 inside. Only two people carry a drawer key. Read the six documents, find the one statement that cannot be true, and name who opened the drawer.

The evidence

Z1 · Office closing sheet

Till counted 5:30 p.m.: $112.40. Placed in safe drawer and locked, 5:31. Drawer keys issued to: N. Ambrose (clerk), H. Cole (inspector). No other copies. — signed N.A.

Z2 · Safe drawer log

5:31 p.m. — opened/closed, deposit — N.A.
5:52 p.m. — opened/closed, “count check” — H.C.
7:00 p.m. — opened, $72.40 found, $40 short — N.A.

Z3 · Statement of Inspector Cole

“I left Elm Street at a quarter past five and went straight to the depot. I was there all evening. I never touched that drawer after lunch. Anybody can scribble ‘H.C.’ in a log book.”

Z4 · Depot telephone register

5:50 p.m. — incoming, from ELM ST OFFICE line — Insp. Cole: “Running late, will be in by half six.”
6:34 p.m. — Insp. Cole signs in at depot desk.

Z5 · Car 4 trip sheet (Conductor Varga)

Outbound 5:40 p.m. from Elm St: 1 fare boarded — N. Ambrose (staff pass), alighted Larch Ave 6:05. Car 4 returned to Elm St 6:50. — P.V.

Z6 · Two more statements

Lou Danby, cleaner: “I swept the office at quarter past six. Drawer was shut and locked. I’ve no key — never had one.”
Sam Ortiz, newsboy: “Miss Ambrose waved at me and got on Car 4 about twenty to six. A while later a lady in an inspector’s cap came out of the office in a hurry. Five to six, maybe.”

Sealed answer (printed upside down; turn the sheet to read it)

Inspector Harriet Cole opened the drawer at 5:52 p.m. with her own key and took $40. The “forged initials” story fails because her own phone call places her in the office at 5:50.

  1. Only Ambrose and Cole hold drawer keys (Z1), and the drawer was opened at 5:52 p.m. (Z2). Danby has no key (Z6); Varga and Ortiz are not key-holders.
  2. Ambrose cannot have opened it at 5:52: she was on Car 4 from 5:40 to 6:05 (Z5), and Ortiz independently saw her board (Z6).
  3. Cole says she was at the depot from 5:15 (Z3). But the depot register logs her calling from the Elm Street office line at 5:50 (Z4), two minutes before the drawer was opened. Her statement is the one that cannot be true.
  4. Ortiz saw a woman in an inspector’s cap hurrying out at about 5:55 (Z6), and Cole did not sign in at the depot until 6:34 (Z4).
